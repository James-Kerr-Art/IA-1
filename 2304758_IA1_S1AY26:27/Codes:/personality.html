Personality

3 of my strongest traits, hobbies etc.

  1. I am quite a stoic person

  2. I create art

  3. I play chess

A stoic person can be described as a person who endures a great deal of hardship with very little complaint, It can also be understood as simply an individual who remains calm during a rough timeRegardless this just means I am able to compose myself very well. I don’t necessarily need to behave this way, it’s just a part of who I am.

When life got harder and my family grew smaller, without that control I would not have been able to resume my studies at the university of technology without some level of handicap or inability.

I create art, I am an artist and how I came to this was due to a simple psychological evaluation, It’s not something I ever planned to do in the future but I simply had a thought. Now I’m doing what I love and even making money from it. It’s why I am here at this institution in the first place.

Lastly, chess, why chess? Chess has been a part of all stages of my life, and everytime I play I always find a way to do something different. It changed the way I think and look towards life.

I realized, I don’t have to necessarily study anything, I just need to make sure I understand and with understanding I can improve there on, I shifted my focus from retention to understanding so even if I forget something specific I have the knowledge to figure it out, it’s equivalent to Not knowing the answer to every math problem but knowing the formula to solve that math problem. A lot of modules I passed or did very well in were simply for that reason.



About My Hero

"The important thing is not to stop questioning". - Albert Einstein

There are a few quotes I wanted to use but I chose this, “ The important thing is not to stop questioning.” Despite the knowledge we have, there is far more of the unknown. The knowledge we possess has made many arrogant with many of those people stuck, restrained by their own understanding. An equivalent to living by an incomplete textbook, knowing all its contents but refusing to fill in its blank pages. If we were all this way we would surely stagnate, so it is important not to just ask questions but to not stop questioning everything around us. Only then can we learn something new.

Born in 1879 passing in 1955, Albert Einstein was a theoretical physicist born in Ulm, Baden-Württemberg Germany. He is renowned for his Theory of Relativity laying the building blocks for Quantum Mechanics, he also proposed and explained photoelectric effect winning him a Nobel Prize while also reshaping our understanding of the celestial entirely.

Albert Einstein in this sense is my hero, very generic I understand but aside from him being “overrated” I do resonate with aspects of him.

Honestly it’s not what he accomplished that I admire, It’s the mindset he had when doing it, He wasn’t actively trying to change the world, he was simply connecting the dots in his spare time and trying to make sense of it, I believe he imagined a fully realized logical world we haven’t even recorded yet and I find that most fascinating.



“LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and CoPilot must be used as a tool not a crutch”.

LLMs have always found a way to rightfully upset me, many are no more than glorified search engines, make constant mistakes and seem to ignore specific or niche instructions in order to compensate for general errors.

LLMs are useful as a tool and should be used to increase productivity, not as a second brain. I notice LLMs have diminished the formal operative thinking of many, though that's just my assumption based on observation.

I believe LLMs should be used to further breakdown or explain material to a student instead of just giving them the answers to everything. LLMs should be used to increase productivity and to aid in problem solving without solely being the productive one and the one solving the problem.

That’s simply how I believe it should be used, I believe when and where would be at home for personal projects or simply to aid in learning though many rather use LLM’s to do the work for them.

In conclusion, LLMs are to be used as simply a tool and not a crutch, our mind is a gift and we should cherish it and use it to the best of our ability.
